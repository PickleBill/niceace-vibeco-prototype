Player promise

No app install

The player experience has to be faster than ordering a drink at the turn: scan, confirm, get entered, watch the pot, invite the foursome.

01QR tee-box entry

Each prize hole has a live URL tied to course, hole, day, prize rules, and pot.

02One-tap payment

$10 entry is the prototype price. Sponsor-paid and hybrid pots can come later.

03Live social proof

Players see field growth, trophy history, and invite actions immediately after joining.

What V1 must not do

Scope discipline
  • Do not start with a full sportsbook or peer-to-peer wagering product.
  • Do not bury prize terms after payment.
  • Do not make operators manage payouts through spreadsheets.
  • Do not sell sponsors vague impressions; sell moments and recap artifacts.

Course operator mode

Pilot product

The course version should be operational: create challenge, select holes, set price/prize rules, start the day, resolve claims, export recap.

Sponsor product

Sell the moment

The sponsor is not buying a banner. They are underwriting the hole everyone talks about, then receiving a branded recap.

Tonight’s $5,000 ace pot is presented by Trophy Room Sports Bar. Scan at Hole 7, take your shot, and bring your entry receipt in for the post-round special.

Sponsor recap

Attribution
  • Entries by event and hole
  • Unique players and repeat entrants
  • Foursome invites and share clicks
  • Coupon/redemption link usage
  • Winner or near-miss social assets

Ace claim path

Trust layer

The claim flow is the product’s credibility engine. It needs to feel celebratory to the player and audit-ready to the operator.

1
Player taps “I aced it”

Locks event, ticket, hole, timestamp, and device context.

2
Evidence checklist

Witnesses, scorecard, optional video, course marshal confirmation.

3
Operator review

Admin resolves claim, triggers payout workflow, creates trophy artifact.

Compliance posture

Before scale

V1 should be framed as sponsor-backed skill-contest entertainment and reviewed by counsel before expanding into peer-funded wagering.

The trust layer ships before the betting layer.
